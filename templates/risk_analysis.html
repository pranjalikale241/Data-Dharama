🤖 Privacy Risk Analysis

AI-assisted analysis to estimate the privacy risk associated with a document access request.

🔐 How it works: The system considers the document sensitivity, requested permission and access duration. A Random Forest machine-learning prototype predicts a risk category, while an explainable rule-based score provides additional context. The result is intended to support the Data Owner's decision and does not automatically approve access.

Risk Factors

📄 Document Sensitivity

Identity and Financial documents receive higher sensitivity weights because they may contain highly sensitive personal information.

🔐 Permission Level

Download access receives a higher risk weight than View Only access because a downloadable copy can leave the controlled system.

⏳ Access Duration

Longer access periods increase the potential exposure window.
