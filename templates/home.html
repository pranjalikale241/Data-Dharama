🌱 About Data Dharma

Data Dharma is a privacy-first, AI-assisted personal data vault and digital identity platform. It allows Data Owners to store documents securely and maintain control over how organizations access them. Organizations can request access for a specific purpose, permission level and duration. The Data Owner remains in control of the final authorization decision, while automatic expiry limits access to the approved time period.
